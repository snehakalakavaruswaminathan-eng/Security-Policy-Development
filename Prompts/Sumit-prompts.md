# Prompt 1

**Date : 26 Jan 2026 - ChatGPT Prompt Optimizer**

## Role & Context

You are drafting an Access Control Policy Framework for a PG-level cybersecurity submission. The initial draft exists but lacks structured hierarchy and governance clarity.

## Task

Refine the raw draft into a structured Access Control Policy document with clear section numbering, professional language, and audit-ready formatting.

## Scope & Requirements

The document must:

- Follow a logical structure (Purpose → Scope → Standards → Governance → Monitoring).
- Remove redundant content and simplify lengthy explanations.
- Convert descriptive paragraphs into enforceable policy statements.
- Align content with ISO 27001 Annex A.9 and NIST SP 800-53.
- Ensure consistent numbering and section alignment.
- Maintain academic tone suitable for professor review.

Include:

- Clear headings and subheadings.
- Bullet points for standards.
- Tables for review cycles and governance mapping.
- Short, precise policy language.

## Tone & Output

Formal, structured, and concise.  
Avoid overly technical jargon.  
Focus on clarity and enforceability.  
Deliver final content ready for academic submission.

# Prompt 2

**Date : 29 Jan 2026 - ChatGPT Prompt Optimizer**

## Role & Context

You are improving the Governance & Maintenance section of an Access Control Policy Framework. The content exists but needs structured compliance mapping and monitoring logic.

## Task

Design a Governance & Compliance section including review cycles, KPIs, exception handling, and audit readiness.

## Scope & Requirements

The section must:

- Define Governance Structure.
- Establish quarterly, semi-annual, and annual review cycles.
- Create a compliance monitoring table.
- Include measurable KPIs (Provisioning time, Recertification rate, Deprovisioning SLA).
- Define Break-Glass and exception management processes.
- Align monitoring practices with NIST SP 800-92 and ISO 27001.

Output must include:

- Review frequency table.
- KPI performance table.
- Clear distinction between Monitoring, Audit, and Regulatory Compliance.
- Concise but professional wording.

## Tone & Output

Professional and audit-ready.  
Structured with headings and tables.  
Minimal narrative — focus on governance clarity.
